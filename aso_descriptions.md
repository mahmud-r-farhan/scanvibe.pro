## Long Description (4000 chars max — Google Play)

**ScanVibe** is the privacy-first document scanner that works entirely on your device. Capture pages with your camera, import images from your gallery, and let on-device AI instantly extract every word — no Wi-Fi, no cloud, no account required.

**📄 Scan Anything, Anywhere**
Point your camera at a receipt, contract, handwritten note, book page, or whiteboard and ScanVibe turns it into a searchable, shareable document in seconds. Works offline — on a plane, in a basement, or wherever you are.

**🔍 On-Device OCR — Powered by Google ML Kit**
Text recognition runs directly on your phone using Google ML Kit. Your images and extracted text never leave your device. No server, no API key, no upload — just instant, private text recognition.

**📑 Export Clean Searchable PDFs**
Combine multiple scanned pages into a single polished PDF with embedded text. Save it to your device and share it wherever you need — email, cloud storage, or messaging apps.

**🌐 Multilingual Interface**
Use ScanVibe in English, Spanish, or Bengali. The language picker appears on first launch and can be changed at any time from Settings — no restart needed.

**🛡️ Built for Privacy**
- Zero internet permission for core features
- No account or sign-up
- No ads
- Images and extracted text stay on your device unless you choose to export them

**✨ Key Features**
- 📷 Camera capture & gallery import
- 🤖 On-device OCR — no internet needed
- 📄 Multi-page document support — add pages to any scan
- 📤 One-tap PDF export to device storage
- 🔄 Retry failed OCR pages individually
- 📊 Per-document OCR progress tracking
- 🌍 English, Spanish & Bengali UI
- 📱 Responsive layout — works on phones and tablets
- 🌙 Clean Material You design

**Perfect for:**
Students, professionals, travelers, journalists, and anyone who needs to digitize documents without trusting a third-party server.

*ScanVibe — scan smarter, stay private.*

## Apple App Store Description (4000 chars max — iOS, optional)

**ScanVibe** is the privacy-first document scanner built for people who don't want their documents uploaded to a server. Capture pages with your camera, import from your photo library, and watch on-device AI extract every word — no Wi-Fi, no subscription, no account.

**Scan. Recognize. Export.**
Point your camera at any document — receipts, contracts, handwritten notes, books, whiteboards — and ScanVibe converts it into a fully searchable PDF in seconds. Everything happens on your device using Apple's and Google's on-device machine learning.

**Why ScanVibe?**
Most scanning apps send your images to a remote server for OCR. ScanVibe does it differently: text recognition runs locally using Google ML Kit. Your documents stay yours.

**Features**
• Camera capture and photo library import
• On-device OCR — works offline, zero uploads
• Multi-page documents — add pages to any scan

• English, Spanish & Bengali interface

• No ads, no account, no subscription
