Privacy Policy for ScanVibe

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

Bengal Bytes ("us", "we", or "our") operates the ScanVibe mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Service").

Core Privacy Value: ScanVibe is built as a fully offline document scanner. All text recognition (OCR), document image rendering, and PDF assembly are performed completely on your local device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your document data.

1. Information Collection and Use

While using our Service, we do not collect any personally identifiable information, usage analytics, or device identifiers. We do not require any user registration or accounts to use the Service.

2. Document Data Security

All scanned document pages, captured camera images, and extracted OCR text remain exclusively on your device. We have no access to your documents. Any PDF export files are saved locally to your device’s document directory, and you have complete control over how and where they are shared.

3. Device Permissions Required

To provide core scanning functionality, the Service requires access to the following hardware features on your device:

  • Camera Permission: Used solely to capture physical documents for scanning and local OCR processing.
  • Storage/Photo Library Access: Used to import existing images from your gallery for scanning and to save/export completed PDF documents to your local storage.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app runs entirely locally and offline. We do not use any third-party analytics tools, ad networks, or cloud tracking databases. No internet connection is required for core scanning, text recognition, or PDF export.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
